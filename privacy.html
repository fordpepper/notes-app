Notes: Privacy policy

Notes is a web app that runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own and no analytics, ads or tracking. Back to the app

Notes and GitHub

Your notes are stored on your device and synced to a GitHub repository that you choose, using an access token that you paste in. The token stays in your browser's local storage. It is only ever sent to GitHub.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you press Add Google account, the app asks Google for two permissions: to list your calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly) and to read and edit events (calendar.events). It uses them only to show your events and to create, change or delete events when you ask it to.

  • Google data and the temporary access token are kept only in your browser (local storage) on your device.
  • They are sent only to Google's own servers. They are never sent to the developer, to any other server or to any third party, and are never used for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • You can remove an account at any time in Settings. This deletes the stored data and revokes the token. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Calendar links and exported settings

Calendar links you add are stored in your own notes repository. A settings file you export is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, on your device.
